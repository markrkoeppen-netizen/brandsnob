About BrandSnobs

You Like What You Like

BrandSnobs started with a simple, specific frustration: a brand I genuinely liked was sold at three different places — a big outdoor retailer, another national sporting goods chain, and directly through the brand's own website. Each one ran its own sales at different times, and there was no easy way to see all of it in one place without manually checking three separate sites, over and over.

That's it. That's the whole idea. Pick the brands you actually care about, and see the best current deals on them — wherever they're being sold — without doing the legwork yourself.

BrandSnobs isn't trying to be a deal site for everyone. It's built around the idea that you already know what you like — we just help you find out when it's on sale.

The site scans across retailers for the brands you follow, surfaces genuine discounts, and links you straight to the actual product — not a search results page, not a homepage, the real thing you'd actually buy.

BrandSnobs is built and maintained independently. If you run into something that doesn't look right, or a deal that doesn't work the way it should, reach out — this is a small, actively-developed project, and feedback genuinely shapes what gets fixed next.
